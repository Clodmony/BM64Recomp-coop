# Player 2 in slot 4, the co-op slot (docs/coop/design.md, *Player 2 in a story
# room*). Slot 4 runs as controlType 2 and reads its command record, which pad
# 1 fills. Green Garden stage 1, room 0x29, entered the way room 0x28's exit
# enters it (entry 0x13); player 2 is placed next to player 1 once its entry
# walk is over (p2-entry-walk, #55).
#env BM64_COOP_WARP=0x29:0x13
#env BM64_COOP_P2=4
#env BM64_COOP_LOG_PLAYERS=1
# A hit knocks a heart out that flies off in a random direction and can be
# caught again (p2-heart, #32); with this seed it is not, so the second bomb
# is a death.
#env BM64_COOP_SEED=1
#
# spawned by the room's own init (an overlay call), with model 0x4B, placed
#check log ^coop: room init
#check log ^coop: stage init overlay=0x29 slot=4 controlType=2 hearts=2
#check log ^coop: spawn slot=4$
#check log ^coop: model slot=4 asset=75$
#check log ^coop: place slot=4 tile=
#check not-after "^coop: probe walked$" "^coop: spawn slot=4$"
#check field start 0 flags = 0
#check field start 4 controlType = 2
#check field start 4 hearts = 2
#check field start 4 flags = 0
#check near start 0 4 200 50
# player 1's first step (down) is +x in this room's camera
#check delta walked start 0 x > 50
# pad 1 moves player 2 and not player 1; pad 0 moves player 1 and not player 2
#check moved p2-0 p2-1 4
#check still p2-0 p2-1 0
#check moved p1-0 p1-1 0
#check still p1-0 p1-1 4
# the command record moves slot 4 the way the pad moves a controlType 1 slot,
# camera-relative (p2-slot1): right is -z, left +z in this room's camera (#13)
#check delta p2-0 p2-1 4 z < -80
#check delta p2-0 p2-1 4 x > -40
#check delta p2-0 p2-1 4 x < 40
#check delta left4-0 left4-1 4 z > 150
#check delta p1-0 p1-1 0 z < -80
# player 2's bomb, its blast takes one of player 2's hearts and not player 1's
#check log ^coop: bomb slot=4 placed=1$
#check after "^coop: bomb slot=4 placed=1" "^coop: hit slot=4 cause="
#check after "^coop: hit slot=4" "^coop: drop slot=4 item=4$"
#check no-log ^coop: pickup slot=4 item=4$
#check field hit1 4 hearts = 1
#check no-log ^coop: hit slot=0
# the second hit is a death: player 2 is down, a ghost, while player 1 plays
# on (no retry, no reload, no life spent; #22, p2-ghost)
#check after "^coop: hit slot=4" "^coop: death slot=4 cause="
#check after "^coop: death slot=4" "^coop: down slot=4 cause=1 lives=2 ghost$"
#check not-after "^coop: death slot=4" "^coop: room teardown"
#check no-log ^coop: death slot=0
#check field down 4 controlType = 2
#check field down 4 ghost = 1
#check field down 0 flags = 0
#check log "^coop: state overlay=0x29 .* lives=2$"
#check no-log "^coop: state overlay=0x29 .* lives=1$"
overlay 0x29
wait 300
probe walked
pad 0 - 0.05 -1
wait 15
pad 0 -
wait 10
probe start
probe p2-0
pad 1 - 1 0.05
wait 15
pad 1 -
wait 10
probe p2-1
probe left4-0
pad 1 - -1 0.05
wait 30
pad 1 -
wait 10
probe left4-1
probe p1-0
pad 0 - 1 0.05
wait 15
pad 0 -
wait 10
probe p1-1
pad 1 A
wait 2
pad 1 -
wait 400
probe hit1
pad 1 A
wait 2
pad 1 -
wait 190
probe down
end
