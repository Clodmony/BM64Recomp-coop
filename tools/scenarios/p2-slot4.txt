# Player 2 in slot 4, the co-op slot (docs/coop/design.md, *Player 2 in a story
# room*). Slot 4 runs as controlType 2 and reads its command record, which pad
# 1 fills. Green Garden stage 1, room 0x29, entered the way room 0x28's exit
# enters it (entry 0x13); player 2 is placed next to player 1 at player 1's
# first step after its entry walk (p2-entry-walk).
#env BM64_COOP_WARP=0x29:0x13
#env BM64_COOP_P2=4
#env BM64_COOP_LOG_PLAYERS=1
#
# spawned by the room's own init (an overlay call), with model 0x4B, placed
#check log ^coop: room init
#check log ^coop: stage init overlay=0x29 slot=4 controlType=2 hearts=2
#check log ^coop: spawn slot=4$
#check log ^coop: model slot=4 asset=75$
#check log ^coop: place slot=4 tile=

#check field start 4 hearts = 2
#check field start 4 flags = 0
#check near start 0 4 150 50
# player 1's first step (down) is +x in this room's camera
#check delta walked start 0 x > 50
# pad 1 moves player 2 and not player 1; pad 0 moves player 1 and not player 2
#check moved p2-0 p2-1 4
#check still p2-0 p2-1 0
#check moved p1-0 p1-1 0
#check still p1-0 p1-1 4

#check delta p2-0 p2-1 4 z < -80

#check delta p2-0 p2-1 4 x < 40

#check delta p1-0 p1-1 0 z < -80
# player 2's bomb, its blast takes one of player 2's hearts and not player 1's
#check log ^coop: bomb slot=4 placed=1$
#check after "^coop: bomb slot=4 placed=1" "^coop: hit slot=4 cause="
#check field hit1 4 hearts = 1
#check no-log ^coop: hit slot=0
# the second hit is a death: player 2 is down while player 1 plays on (no
# retry, no reload), then respawns next to player 1 with full hearts for one
# of the shared lives (#18), at player 1's next step
#check after "^coop: hit slot=4" "^coop: death slot=4 cause="
#check after "^coop: death slot=4" "^coop: down slot=4 cause=1 lives=2$"
#check after "^coop: down slot=4" "^coop: respawn slot=4 hearts=2 lives=1$"
#check after "^coop: respawn slot=4" "^coop: place slot=4 tile="
#check not-after "^coop: death slot=4" "^coop: room teardown"
#check no-log ^coop: death slot=0
